Agent product marks
===================

hide bundles two small product marks so the sidebar can say which tool is
running in a pane:

  Sources/HerdrMacOS/Resources/agent-claude.png  - Claude Code, Anthropic PBC
  Sources/HerdrMacOS/Resources/agent-codex.png   - Codex, OpenAI, Inc.

These marks are used only to identify the corresponding product in hide's
interface. They are the property of their respective owners, hide claims no
rights in them, and their presence does not imply that either company
endorses, sponsors, or is affiliated with hide.

Neither mark is used as hide's own branding, and neither appears in the
application icon.

To remove them, delete the two files above; the sidebar falls back to the
agent's initial and nothing else changes.
