Agent product marks
===================

hide bundles small product marks of the coding agents it works with, so its
interface can say which tool is running in a pane and which agents the
first-run choice offers:

  web/src/assets/agent-claude.png  - Claude Code, Anthropic PBC
  web/src/assets/agent-codex.png   - Codex, OpenAI, Inc.
  web/src/assets/agents/           - the other agents' marks; manifest.json
                                     there names each file's product, source,
                                     terms and retrieval date

These marks are used only to identify the corresponding product in hide's
interface. They are the property of their respective owners, hide claims no
rights in them, and their presence does not imply that any owner endorses,
sponsors, or is affiliated with hide.

No mark is used as hide's own branding, and none appears in the application
icon.

To remove a mark under web/src/assets/agents/, delete its file and move its
manifest entry to the monogram list; the first-run choice then draws the
agent's initials and nothing else changes.
